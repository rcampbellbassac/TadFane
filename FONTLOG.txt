FONTLOG for Tad Fane

Tad Fane (ຕາດຟານ) is a display font for Lao, Thai, Khmer and Latin drawn from a hand-painted
sign photographed in Laos in 2019. One family from two photos of the same Tad Fane resort sign (studies 06 and 07, pack 26 and 27): extra-heavy rounded Lao with big round counters. Latin follows the sign's separate bold serif (Noto Serif base), not the Lao outline genre.

How it was made
- Lao key letters (ຕ າ ດ ຟ ນ ຣ ີ ສ ອ) were traced from the photograph (scripts/segment.py, scripts/trace.py).
- The other letters were generated on the Noto designs with a simulated brush whose size and the font's width were
  measured from the traced letters (scripts/auto.py), then merged with the traced letters (scripts/hybrid.py).
- Generated letters are drafts to be redrawn toward the traced ones before 1.0.
